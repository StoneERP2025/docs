Everything in Fail Fast starts from the sidebar. It has three areas: the workspace dropdown at the top, a set of fixed items, and the **Programs** section with your workspace's pages.

## Workspace dropdown

The dropdown at the top of the sidebar is your company switcher and account hub. From here you can:

- **Switch companies** — pick any other company your account belongs to.
- **Configuration** — open Settings for the current workspace in a dialog.
- **Invite Teammates** — send invitations to new members (administrators only).
- See the workspace's **member count**.
- **Create a company** — start a new workspace. See [Create or join a workspace](/getting-started/create-your-workspace).
- **Logout** — sign out of your account.

## Fixed sidebar items

These items are always in the same place, though some are hidden depending on who you are and what your workspace has enabled.

| Item | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | The AI assistant — your starting point. See [AI assistant](/ai/ai-assistant). | Everyone |
| **Search** | Opens a command palette to jump to pages and records from anywhere. | Everyone |
| **Notifications** | Opens the notifications panel; a badge shows your unread count. | Everyone |
| **Workflows** | Your workspace's automations. See [Workflows](/automation/workflows). | Hidden for guests |
| **Agent Tasks** | Tracks background jobs so you can follow long-running work. | Hidden for guests |
| **Calendar** | Scheduled events and appointments. See [Calendar and tasks](/platform/calendar-and-tasks). | Everyone |
| **Chats** | WhatsApp conversations. | Only when WhatsApp is enabled for your workspace |
| **Invite Teammates** | Send invitations to join the workspace. | Administrators |

## The Programs section

**Programs** is the tree of pages configured for your workspace — this is where your business modules and day-to-day screens live. See [Programs and pages](/platform/programs-and-pages) for the full guide.

In the tree you can:

- **Drag items** to reorder them or nest one under another.
- **Collapse all** branches with the hover control on the section header.
- Use the **+** menu next to the header to **Create page** or **Create workflow**.
- Open each item's context menu for **Permissions** and **Delete** — both are permission-gated.

Items can be editable or view-only for you, depending on your permissions. What appears in the tree varies by workspace: enabled applications, your company's template, and your role all shape it.

## Guests

Guests see a reduced interface: **Workflows** and **Agent Tasks** are hidden, and they only reach the pages shared with them. Roles and visibility are covered in [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
