## What is Fail Fast?

Fail Fast is an all-in-one no-code business platform and ERP. Your company runs as a **workspace**, and everyday work is organized into **Programs** — pages built from tables, forms, views, and dashboards that your team configures without writing code.

On top of that foundation, Fail Fast gives you:

- **Built-in AI assistance** — the Home screen is an AI assistant that helps you find, create, and understand your data.
- **Visual workflow automation** — build automations that react to events in your workspace and connect to outside services.
- **Business modules** — CRM, sales, purchasing, inventory, finance, and transport, delivered as Programs pages over your tables and forms, if enabled for your workspace.
- **Colombian market support** — DIAN electronic invoicing and the RNDC transport registry are built in for companies operating in Colombia.

Because modules are configurations of the same building blocks, no two workspaces look exactly alike. What you see depends on your company's template, enabled applications, and your role and permissions.

## Who these docs are for

These guides are written for the people who use Fail Fast every day: end users working with records and forms, workspace administrators managing teams and permissions, and operations teams running business processes.
