When the trip ends, three closings remain: the cumplido (the compliment), which proves the trip took place; the legalization, which settles the vehicle owner; and billing, which charges the customer. The last two end in an invoice: a supplier invoice (or a support document) in payables and a customer invoice in receivables.

The transport screens are in Spanish, so labels appear here exactly as on screen.

## Where to find it

Go to **TMS** > **Transporte Nacional** > **Transacciones**. There you find **Cumplido** and **Facturación**.

## Keep in mind

- The cumplido starts from a confirmed manifest. See [Transport operations](/transport/operations).
- Each side follows its own sequence: the cumplido is registered before legalizing, and remittances exist before billing.
- The legalization and billing operations are the ones you picked in [Set up transport](/transport/setup).
- Fields marked **Requerido** are mandatory.

## Cumplido

The cumplido is the proof that a manifest's trip took place. It is registered per manifest, with the detail per remittance and the settlement concepts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Transacciones** > **Cumplido** and select **Nuevo**. The tabs are **Operación**, **Detalle del cumplido**, and **Concepto de liquidación**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the operation">
    Pick the **Operación** and **Número de Operación**, the **Fecha de Operación**, the **Sucursal**, and the **Centro de Costos**. All are required. **Moneda**, **Descripción**, and **Tasa de Cambio de Mercado** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say how the service went">
    **Cumplido estandar** (required) shows whether the service was normal (**Sí**) or was suspended by an incident (**No**). If you mark it as non-standard, **Causa de la suspension** (for example, accident, incident, or breakdown) and **Consecuencia** (the measure taken, for example, driver change or end of trip) become mandatory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the detail and the concepts">
    In **Detalle del cumplido**, review what was fulfilled for each remittance. In **Concepto de liquidación**, review the concepts added to or subtracted from the carrier's payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and report to RNDC">
    Confirm the cumplido. Then select **Enviar remesa RNDC** and **Enviar manifiesto RNDC**. The list keeps the result in **Remesa RNDC enviada** and **Manifiesto RNDC enviado**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Esta legalizado** (required) starts at **No**. It shows whether the cumplido was already legalized. Later, the **Estado de la remesa** moves from **Generado** to **Cumplido**.

## Legalization

Legalization is the settlement with the vehicle owner.

Legalization:

- Consolidates the owner's cumplidos that are pending legalization.
- Generates the supplier invoice, or the support document if the owner does not invoice. It uses the legalization operation from [branch configuration](/transport/setup) that matches each case.
- Applies the owner's withholdings and leaves the payable at the net value.
- Closes the manifest's transit in suppliers. See [Remittances and manifests in transit](/transport/transit).

To see a legalization's entries with real numbers, see the [full example](/transport/example-trip).

## Billing

Billing groups remittances to charge them to the customer. When you confirm it, it generates the customer invoice and closes the manifest debit account of each remittance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Transacciones** > **Facturación** and select **Nuevo**. The form has the **Información general** tab and the **Contabilización NIIF** and **Contabilización fiscal** tabs. Inside, the tabs **Operación**, **Resumen**, **Remesa**, **Adicional**, **Conceptos**, and **Impuesto**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the operation">
    Pick the **Operación** and **Número de Operación**, the **Fecha de Operación**, and the **Sucursal**. All are required. **Moneda**, **Descripción**, and **Tasa de Cambio de Mercado** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the customer and how to group">
    **Cliente** and **Detalle del Cliente** are required. **Modo de agrupación** sets whether one record is generated per customer (**CLIENTE**) or one per customer and load order (**ORDEN DE CARGA**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in the remittances">
    The **Remesa** tab shows the remittances being billed. The form has the **Obtener el encabezado de la remesa de facturación** button and the **Importar** button. The **Adicional** tab shows the additional charges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the totals">
    In **Resumen**, review **Total de Tarifa del Cliente** (what you charge), **Total del flete del transportador** (what you pay), **Ganancia total de remesas**, **Rentabilidad total de remesas**, the additional income and expenses, the incremental and decremental taxes, the **Beneficio total**, the **Rentabilidad total**, and the **Número de remesas**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the billing. When it finishes, **Descripción del Estado** shows **Facturación procesada.** and the **Factura** field shows the generated invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The form has the **PDF** button. **Notas** holds the billing's comments.

<Warning>
While the billing is **Provisional**, the remittances stay in transit as **Tránsito** (in transit) rows in customer aging. They leave on the date of the invoice that bills them.
</Warning>

## Where the money goes next

The invoices generated here become open balances: the customer invoice adds to your receivables and the supplier invoice to your payables. Both are collected or paid later from [Treasury](/finance/treasury): income records settle the customer's balance and payments settle the owner's.

## In transit in the aging reports

Between the trip and its invoices, the freight is already in the ledger: confirming the manifest posts it. The customer and supplier aging reports show it as **Tránsito** rows until the sales invoice and the legalization close it. See [Remittances and manifests in transit](/transport/transit) for what each document posts, how it shows in aging, and a full example.
