Transport has four reports of its own, with filters by period, branch, business line, customer, and plate, and two aging reports that show receivables and payables, including freight in transit.

The transport screens are in Spanish, so labels appear here exactly as on screen.

## Where to find it

- The four dashboards are in **TMS** > **Transporte Nacional** > **Reportes**.
- **Edades de Clientes** (customer aging) and **Edades de Proveedores** (supplier aging) are in **ERP**.

## Keep in mind

- When there is no data for the period and filters you chose, each chart shows **No hay datos disponibles para esta gráfica.**
- All dashboards filter by **Periodo** (**Desde** and **Hasta**), **Sucursal**, **Línea de negocio**, **Cliente**, and **Placa del vehículo**.

## The four dashboards

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Tablero gerencial de transporte">
    The management view: how much comes in, how much it costs, and who owes.

    - **Resumen del periodo.**
    - **Evolución mensual**: income, cost, and profit per month, and remittances per month.
    - **Clientes y rutas**: top 10 customers by income, income by business line, and top 10 routes by remittances.
    - **Participación**: by customer, by branch, and by business line.
    - **Cartera y cuentas por pagar**: receivables aging, aging of what is payable to holders, and receivables by customer.
    - **Detalle**: service orders of the period.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tablero de operación de transporte">
    The day-to-day view: which step each trip is at.

    - **Resumen del periodo.**
    - **Avance de la cadena**: of the holder and of the customer, open chains by pending step, and average days per leg.
    - **Reporte al RNDC**: status of the RNDC report.
    - **Flota**: trips per vehicle and freight per holder.
    - **Detalle**: remittances and their status.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rentabilidad y servicio de transporte">
    The profitability and customer service view.

    - **Rentabilidad**: profit and margin by customer, by business line, and by route, and income and cost per ton by route.
    - **Concentración**: the top 5 customers and the top 5 carriers.
    - **Servicio**: appointment compliance by customer and shortfalls by holder.
    - **Uso de la flota**: trips per month and vehicles without trips per month.
    - **Dinero**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sucursales de transporte">
    The comparison between branches.

    - **Resultado**: result by branch, ranking by margin, and monthly trend.
    - **Volumen y cadena**: volume by branch, ranking by cycle in days, and open chains by branch and step.
    - **Dinero**: advances and payables, receivables, and receivables and cumplido not yet billed, all by branch.
    - **Reporte al RNDC**: RNDC report by branch.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Aging reports

**Edades de Clientes** and **Edades de Proveedores** show open balances by age. While a trip is not billed or legalized, its freight appears as a **Tránsito** (in transit) row: one per remittance in customers and one per manifest in suppliers. See [Remittances and manifests in transit](/transport/transit) for how each row is calculated and when it leaves. The [full example](/transport/example-trip) shows the effect date by date.
