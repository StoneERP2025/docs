Open **Settings → Workspace → Settings** to manage the workspace itself — its identity, configuration, and subscription. This page is admin-only.

## Workspace Settings

The **Workspace Settings** section covers your company's identity and configuration:

- **Logo** — upload your company logo.
- **Name** — edit the workspace name.
- Workspace configuration toggles that adjust how the workspace behaves.
- Stakeholder tree configuration — use **Open stakeholder tree** to work with it.

## Subscription

You can view your subscription status from this page. If the subscription is scheduled for cancellation, click **Continue Subscription** to reactivate it and keep the workspace running.

<Warning>
An active subscription is required to use the workspace. If it lapses, a dialog prompts you to reactivate before you can continue working.
</Warning>

## Danger Zone

The **Danger Zone** holds the actions you should think twice about:

- **Cancel Subscription** — schedule the subscription to end.
- **Delete Company** — delete the workspace.

<Warning>
Deleting the company is destructive. Make sure this is really what you want before you proceed.
</Warning>

## Why workspaces differ

<Note>
Your workspace was initialized from an industry template with a set of enabled applications. That's why the **Programs** menus differ between companies — two workspaces on Fail Fast can look quite different depending on the template and applications they started from. See [create your workspace](/getting-started/create-your-workspace) for how this happens at setup.
</Note>

## Common tasks

### Update the logo and name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → Settings** (admin-only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the logo">
    In **Workspace Settings**, upload your company **Logo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    Update the **Name** so the workspace reflects your company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage the subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → Settings** and review your subscription status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reactivate if needed">
    If the subscription is scheduled for cancellation, click **Continue Subscription** to keep it active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel deliberately">
    To end the subscription, use **Cancel Subscription** in the **Danger Zone** — remembering that an active subscription is required to use the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Workspace settings shape the environment; people shape the work. Pair this page with [team and invitations](/admin/team-and-invitations) to keep both in order.
</Tip>
