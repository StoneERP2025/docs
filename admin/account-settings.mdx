Your personal settings live in **Settings**, under the **Account** section. To open Settings, click the workspace dropdown at the top of the sidebar and choose **Configuration**. Account settings apply to you — they follow your account, not the workspace.

## My profile

The **My profile** page holds your identity and sign-in details.

### Profile

- Update your avatar.
- Edit your full name.

### Account security

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Use **Change email** to update the email address on your account. |
| **Phone** | Use **Change phone** to update your phone number. |
| **Password** | Use **Set password** to create a password. Once set, you can sign in with email and password alongside Google or Microsoft. |

### Account management

<Warning>
**Delete my account** permanently deletes your account. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Language

The **Language** page changes the language used in the system. You can choose between **English** and **Español**. The language is also selectable at login, so you can pick it before you sign in.

<Note>
The language setting applies to the app interface for your account. Other people in the workspace keep their own language choice.
</Note>

## Common tasks

### Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the workspace dropdown at the top of the sidebar and choose **Configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your profile">
    In the **Account** section, open **My profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the password">
    Under account security, use **Set password**. With a password set, you can sign in with email and password as well as Google or Microsoft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Switch language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the workspace dropdown at the top of the sidebar and choose **Configuration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Language">
    In the **Account** section, open **Language**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your language">
    Choose **English** or **Español**. The interface updates for your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>
