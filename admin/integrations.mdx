Open **Settings → Workspace → Integrations** to connect your workspace with external services. Each integration on this page connects a workspace-wide capability — set it up once and the related features light up across the app.

### Electronic Documents

The **Electronic Documents** integration connects your DIAN credentials so your workspace can issue electronic invoices and documents. Opening it starts the setup wizard, which walks you through:

- Uploading your certificate
- Syncing your DIAN resolutions
- Enabling automatic accrual (causación)
- Synchronizing master data

Once connected, electronic invoicing is available across your finance operations. The full guide is at [electronic invoicing](/finance/electronic-invoicing).

### WhatsApp Business

The **WhatsApp Business** integration connects your WhatsApp Business account to enable the Chats inbox, where your team handles WhatsApp conversations inside Fail Fast.

<Info>
This integration is shown only when WhatsApp is available for your workspace.
</Info>

The full guide is at [WhatsApp chats](/ai/whatsapp-chats).

## Other places things connect

<Note>
Not every connection lives on the Integrations page:

- **Workflow credentials** are configured inside workflow nodes — see [credentials](/automation/credentials).
- **Email accounts** are configured in the Email Config tool.
- **Data mapping** between your data and external systems happens in the homologation screens — see [data homologation](/data/data-homologation).

If you're looking for a connection and don't find it here, check the feature it belongs to.
</Note>
