These examples show what Fail Fast settles with the complete configuration. The figures for the four sales match an accountant's calculation, to the peso. The sales are by a technology company in Bogotá, November 2026, with the line location in Bogotá.

**The selling company:**

- VAT-responsible company, legal entity, and income tax filer.
- Principal CIIU 6201, with 6202 and 6311.
- Its principal CIIU is in a 1.1 % self-withholding group.
- It has an account map with VAT, income withholding, ReteICA, ReteIVA, and self-withholding, the latter with a counter account.

**The customers** are companies that are income withholding and ICA withholding agents in Bogotá. The fourth customer is also a VAT withholding agent.

## Sale 1: consulting for 20,000,000

A company customer. Product: consulting, with VAT at the general rate and fees withholding. The customer has a ReteICA exception at 6.9 per thousand registered. See [Rule exceptions](/finance/tax-rule-exceptions).

| Item | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 19 % | **3,800,000** (adds to the total) |
| Income withholding (fees, legal entity) | 11 % | **2,200,000** (subtracts from the total) |
| Bogotá ReteICA | 6.9 per thousand | **138,000** (subtracts from the total) |
| Special income self-withholding | 1.1 % | **220,000** (not in the total) |

Total to collect: `20,000,000 + 3,800,000 − 2,200,000 − 138,000 = 21,462,000`.

## Sale 2: consulting for 5,000,000

Another company customer, with the same 6.9 per thousand exception.

| Item | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 19 % | **950,000** |
| Income withholding | 11 % | **550,000** |
| Bogotá ReteICA | 6.9 per thousand | **34,500** |
| Special income self-withholding | 1.1 % | **55,000** |

Total to collect: `5,000,000 + 950,000 − 550,000 − 34,500 = 5,365,500`.

## Sale 3: cloud software for 1,750,000

Product: cloud software. The tax category is **VAT excluded** and the withholding category is data processing.

| Item | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 0 % (excluded) | **0** |
| Income withholding (data processing) | 3.5 % | **61,250** |
| Bogotá ReteICA (services, no exception) | 9.66 per thousand | **16,905** |
| Special income self-withholding | 1.1 % | **19,250** |

Total to collect: `1,750,000 − 61,250 − 16,905 = 1,671,845`.

## Sale 4: development for 2,538,000

The customer is a company, an income, VAT, and ICA withholding agent in Bogotá.

| Item | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 19 % | **482,220** |
| ReteIVA (on the VAT) | 15 % | **72,333** |
| Income withholding (fees) | 11 % | **279,180** |
| Bogotá ReteICA (services) | 9.66 per thousand | **24,517** (24,517.08 before rounding) |
| Special income self-withholding | 1.1 % | **27,918** |

Total to collect: `2,538,000 + 482,220 − 72,333 − 279,180 − 24,517 = 2,644,190`.

<Note>
The self-withholding is a cost of your own: it goes to the map's accounts, outside the total you collect. And the system rounds each tax to the peso.
</Note>

### Why the figures change if you change a piece of data

| If… | What changes |
|---|---|
| The customer is not listed as an income withholding agent | There is no income withholding |
| The customer is not listed as a ReteICA agent in Bogotá | There is no ReteICA |
| There is no 6.9 per thousand exception | ReteICA on sales 1 and 2 is 193,200 and 48,300 (9.66 per thousand) |
| Your company has no principal CIIU | There is no self-withholding |

## Partial return in receivables

A return **does not recalculate** taxes: it takes the returned proportion of each line (returned quantity divided by sold quantity) and applies it to what the original invoice settled. All values are positive; what changes is the accounting side.

To return, the invoice has to be **processed**, the return date cannot be earlier than the invoice's, and the quantity cannot exceed the sold one.

40 % of the quantity of sale 1 is returned with a credit note:

| Item | Invoice | 40 % returned |
|---|---|---|
| Subtotal | 20,000,000 | **8,000,000** |
| VAT | 3,800,000 | **1,520,000** |
| Income withholding | 2,200,000 | **880,000** |
| ReteICA | 138,000 | **55,200** |
| Self-withholding | 220,000 | **88,000** |
| Credit note total | 21,462,000 | **8,584,800** |

The entry reverses the revenue, the receivable, and each tax. The self-withholding is reversed through the return account of its map row. If a return account of the product type or of the map is missing, the return tells you. See [Tax account map](/finance/tax-account-map).

## Partial return at the point of sale

A point-of-sale return also prorates the original sale.

Illustration: a sale of 3 units at 100,000 with 19 % VAT settled `300,000 + 57,000 = 357,000`. One unit is returned: a third of each amount.

| Item | Sale | 1 of 3 returned |
|---|---|---|
| Subtotal | 300,000 | **100,000** |
| VAT | 57,000 | **19,000** |
| Total | 357,000 | **119,000** |

The return is rejected if the product type lacks the sales return, cost, and inventory accounts, or if a tax's map row has no return account. The refund comes out of cash or bank.

## A purchase: a commerce product from a Bogotá supplier

Your company is an income and ICA withholding agent in Bogotá. The supplier files income tax, is VAT responsible, and is not a self-withholder. Product: goods taxed at the general rate, with the purchase-of-goods withholding. Amount 2,000,000, with the line location in Bogotá.

| Item | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 19 % | **380,000** (deductible or to cost, according to the map) |
| Income withholding (purchases, filer) | 2.5 % | **50,000** (the minimum is 10 UVT = 523,740) |
| ReteIVA | — | **0** (your company is not a VAT withholding agent) |
| Bogotá ReteICA (commerce, group) | 6.9 per thousand | **13,800** (the minimum is 27 UVT = 1,414,098) |

You pay the supplier `2,000,000 + 380,000 − 50,000 − 13,800 = 2,316,200`.

Variants with the same purchase:

| Variant | Result |
|---|---|
| The supplier is an income self-withholder | Income withholding 0. You pay 2,366,200 |
| The purchase is for 1,000,000 | ReteICA 0, because it does not reach 27 UVT. Income withholding is 25,000 |
| The supplier declared its ICA activity and is bimonthly, and its activity rate is 4 per thousand | ReteICA of 8,000 instead of 13,800, once the activity rate is in force. See [CIIU, ICA activity, and ReteICA](/finance/tax-ica-activity) |
| The supplier self-withholds ICA in Bogotá | ReteICA 0 |
| The supplier has no data on whether it files income tax | It is treated as a non-filer: income withholding is 3.5 %, 70,000 |

2026 UVT: 52,374.
