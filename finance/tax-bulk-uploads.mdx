The bulk uploads for **suppliers**, **customers**, and **patients** carry the `ciiu` column. With it you load each stakeholder's CIIU without opening their record. To load the rest of the tax profile, use the tax data upload.

## The CIIU column

The `ciiu` cell is a **list**: it goes between brackets and quotes, with the codes separated by commas.

| What you load | How the cell is written |
|---|---|
| One CIIU | `["6201"]` |
| Several CIIU | `["6201","6202"]` |
| Person with no CIIU | An empty cell. For natural persons the CIIU is optional |

The tax responsibilities column, `fiscal_responsibility`, is also a list: for example `["O-13"]` or `["R-99-PN"]`.

## The principal CIIU

| What you load | What happens to the principal |
|---|---|
| A single CIIU | It is marked principal on its own |
| Several CIIU and the stakeholder already had a principal that is still in the list | The principal it already had is kept |
| Several CIIU and the stakeholder had no principal | Mark the principal in their record after the upload: the screen asks for it on their next edit |

The upload does not delete the principal CIIU the stakeholder already had. Stakeholders with several CIIU and no principal show in the [Pending report](/finance/tax-verify#pending-report).

## Rules by type of stakeholder

- **Companies (suppliers and customers with a NIT):** the CIIU is required, except for foreign non-residents, who are identified by their document type (NITE, TDE, DIE, passport) or by belonging to another country.
- **Natural persons (ID card, and patients):** the CIIU is optional.
- **Foreign non-residents:** the CIIU is not required.

## What the upload rejects

When a row cannot be loaded, the message starts with "Row N:" and says why.

## Tax data upload

The tax data upload fills in the Tax profile of several stakeholders at once. It rejects:

- A row for a data point the system calculates (`persona_juridica` or `extranjero`).
- A zone on a country-wide data point.

See [Tax profile](/finance/tax-fiscal-profile).
