The **Tax account map** (**Mapa de cuentas de impuestos**) says, for each tax and each role it plays, which accounting account it goes to. With it, Fail Fast builds the entry of a document with its taxes.

Each row of the map has:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Tax** | The tax or withholding: VAT, income withholding, ReteICA, ReteIVA, self-withholding |
| **Role** | What the account does in that operation (see the table below) |
| **Account** | The accounting account where the tax is recorded |
| **Return account** | The account a return uses to reverse this tax |
| **Counter account** | The account on the other side, only for taxes outside the document's total |
| **Zone** | The municipality where the row is valid. Leave it empty for the row to be valid across the country |

Rows follow the rule on their own when it changes version. You do not have to load them again.

## The account roles

| Tax | Role | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | **Deductible** | The VAT you pay and then deduct |
| VAT | **Higher cost value** | The VAT you do not deduct |
| VAT | **Generated** | The VAT you charge on your sales |
| Income withholding | **Withheld** | What you withhold from the supplier and owe to the DIAN |
| Income withholding | **Suffered** | What is withheld from you on your sales |
| ICA withholding | **Withheld** / **Suffered** | The same, with the municipality |
| VAT withholding | **Withheld** / **Suffered** | Only if you are a VAT withholding agent, or if you are withheld from |
| Special income self-withholding | **Generated** | See the warning below |

Fill in the **zone** only when you need a different account per municipality, which is normal for ICA.

Accounts that carry taxes must have **Requires tax** set to **Yes** and their tax concept. See [Create an account](/finance/accounting#create-an-account).

<Warning>
**Special income self-withholding.** Its role is **Generated**, not **Withheld**. The **account** (credit) is the liability payable to the DIAN, for example `23657501`. The **counter account** (debit) is the advance or prepaid tax, for example `13551501`. The **return account** is the same liability account. If you swap the role or invert the accounts, the sale, point of sale included, is rejected with the notice that the account with role **Generated** is missing.
</Warning>

## The counter account

A tax that is outside the document's total, such as the self-withholding, needs an account and a **counter account**: the entry carries the tax amount in the account, against the counter account, outside the total you collect. Without a counter account, the document is rejected.

The counter account goes on the opposite side from the account. If the account is a credit, the counter account is a debit.

## The map's return account and the product type's

They are two different accounts, and both are needed to return:

| | Where it is configured | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **The map's return account** | Tax account map, in each tax's row | Reverses the **tax** (VAT, withholding) of what is returned |
| **The product type's return accounts** | Product type | Reverses the **revenue**, the cost, and the inventory of what is returned. See [Products](/finance/tax-products) |

A return does not recalculate taxes: it takes the returned proportion of each line and applies it to what the original invoice settled. All values are positive and what changes is the accounting side. If a row's return account is missing, the return, point-of-sale returns included, is rejected.

## When an account is missing

If a tax that was calculated is missing its account, you do not lose the document: the tax is calculated and stored, but the document does not post. The message says:

- the **rule** whose account is missing,
- the tax **concept**,
- the account **role**,
- the **side** that is missing, credit or debit,
- the **zone**,
- and how to fix it.

If what is missing is the counter account, the message says the counter account is missing and the side it goes on, the opposite of the account's.

**Where to fix it:** add or correct the row in the **Tax account map**, with the role and side the message gives, and post again.

<Tip>
Do not wait for a document to fail. The [Pending report](/finance/tax-verify#pending-report) lists the companies that are subject to a rule with an accounting role and have no row in the map, or have the row without a counter account.
</Tip>

## The minimum to start

| You want to… | Row you need |
|---|---|
| Buy with deductible VAT | VAT · Deductible |
| Buy with VAT that goes to cost | VAT · Higher cost value |
| Withhold income tax from the supplier | Income withholding · Withheld |
| Withhold ICA | ICA withholding · Withheld, with zone |
| Sell with VAT | VAT · Generated |
| Record what is withheld from you | Income withholding / ReteICA / ReteIVA · Suffered |
| Self-withhold income tax | Self-withholding · Generated, with counter account and return account |
